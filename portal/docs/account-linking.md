# Administrative OIDC account linking and merging

Accounts & roles includes **Link or merge OIDC accounts** for administrators with global `user.manage` and `role.manage`. Use another administrator to change your own account. Keep a usable local recovery administrator.

1. Independently verify the exact issuer URL and immutable subject (`sub`) in the identity provider. A matching name or email is never sufficient.
2. Select the existing local account as the survivor so its password, local login method, username and history remain available. To pre-link, leave the duplicate account blank. To merge, select the duplicate OIDC account and enter its verified identity.
3. Review identities, enabled status, roles, permission lists, each role's scope/source, owned scans and historical references. Select every durable local or CATS-managed assignment to retain. Survivor durable assignments are initially selected; source permissions are never automatically combined. Conflicting grant sources for the same scope must be resolved explicitly.
4. Type the displayed confirmation and apply within ten minutes. Any intervening account, access or ownership change requires another preview. Sessions and pending imports for both accounts are revoked. Sign in again with either the retained local password or the OIDC identity.

OIDC-derived roles/eligibility are cleared and re-evaluated against current validated claims on the next OIDC login; they cannot be converted into permanent local permissions by a merge. A disabled account stays disabled. A disabled source cannot be merged into an enabled survivor. Retired accounts cannot sign in, be reenabled or reset; merge participants remain retained so historical actor references cannot be destroyed by account deletion.

Merges retain source usernames and historical audit, request, approval and execution actor references. Completed scan ownership transfers transactionally, retaining `original_owner_user_id`. Finish or cancel active scans before merging. Pending import previews are revoked because they were approved under the old identity. The audit records the administrator, source and survivor IDs, identity digest and issuer, previous assignments and exact approved assignments; it contains no passwords, authorization codes or tokens.

Three additive tables (`oidc_account_identities`, `account_retirements`, `account_change_previews`) are created by the existing startup schema upgrade. Existing issuer/subject hashes and explicitly configured account links continue to work. Verified logins add the readable issuer/subject identity record. No accounts are linked by a migration. If an old environment/configuration `account_links` entry still names a retired duplicate, explicitly update that entry to the survivor: contradictory administrative bindings fail closed. No additional environment variables or Compose services are required; deploy the rebuilt image with the existing database volume and take the normal database backup first.

Acceptance: pre-link a test identity and verify local and OIDC sign-in resolve to one ID; merge a duplicate after reviewing different grants; confirm old source history retains its actor, old sessions fail, and only selected durable access remains. Verify disabled-source rejection, identity collision, stale preview, self-change rejection and retention of the final password-backed administrator. These workflows have local regression coverage; a real provider should be used for deployment acceptance.
